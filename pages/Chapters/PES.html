About Us

IEEE PES at Tafila Technical University focuses on advancing knowledge and awareness in power systems and energy technologies. The chapter targets innovation, sustainability, and professional excellence by supporting technical development and promoting modern energy solutions that meet current and future challenges.

🎯

Our Mission

Empowering students in power systems and renewable energy while building professional and leadership skills.

👥

Community

Building a diverse network of passionate engineers and innovators

🚀

Innovation

Fostering creativity and cutting-edge technical projects

Our Activities

💻

Technical Workshops

Technical workshops focused on power systems, renewable energy, smart grids, and electrical system analysis, combining theoretical knowledge with hands-on practical experience using modern tools and software.

🏆

Competitions

Organizing and participating in engineering competitions, hackathons, and technical challenges related to the energy sector, aiming to enhance problem-solving skills, innovation, and teamwork.

🤝

Industry & Academic Engagement

Organizing field visits to energy companies and power stations, technical talks, and meetings with industry professionals and academic experts to bridge the gap between students and the energy job market.
